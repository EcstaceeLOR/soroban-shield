# Code of conduct

SorobanShield is a security-related project, and good security work depends on people feeling able to ask questions, report mistakes and disagree respectfully.

## Expectations

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Be precise about uncertainty. Do not overstate what the scanner detects or what a finding means.
- Welcome newcomers. Explain reasoning in reviews so others can learn from it.
- Do not harass, demean or discriminate against anyone.
- Do not publish exploit details for third-party projects before coordinating disclosure with their maintainers.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers using the contact route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), and mark the report as a conduct concern rather than a vulnerability. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions or restrict participation when these expectations are not met.
